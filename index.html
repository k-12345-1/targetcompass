A proposed Pfizer pilot · People, process and safeguards

How Pfizer could use it safely, without letting AI make the decision.

A practical pilot design that Pfizer could adapt to its existing review process. TargetCompass gathers and explains the evidence; Pfizer experts challenge it and make the final decision. The first step would be one small UC pilot.

A simple four-step review

Every step has a person responsible and leaves a record that can be checked later.

Step 1

Collect the evidence

Record every source and date. Clearly show what is missing or disagrees.

A DATA OWNER CHECKS IT
Step 2

Challenge the science

Scientists ask: is the target really causal, and are we trying to drug it in the right direction?

SCIENTISTS SIGN OFF
Step 3

Check risk and value

Safety, clinical and business experts test whether it could help patients and make strategic sense.

EXPERTS REVIEW TOGETHER
Step 4

Make the decision

A Pfizer committee chooses GO, HOLD or STOP and records why.

PEOPLE DECIDE, NOT AI

Proposed value framework · How better choices save money

The biggest value is choosing better R&D bets earlier.

TargetCompass can save preparation time, but that is only the beginning. The larger opportunity is to stop weak ideas before more money is spent, focus the next experiment on the biggest uncertainty, and notice promising targets before competitors make them expensive.

Four ways the product could create value

Measure each one separately so the same benefit is not counted twice.

01 · Time

Prepare reviews faster

Reusable evidence packs give scientists and analysts more time for experiments and judgment.

  • Measure: hours per review
  • Who checks it: R&D operations
  • Can be tested in the pilot
02 · Avoided spending

Stop weak ideas earlier

Contradictory evidence, safety problems and overlap with existing Pfizer programs appear before avoidable work begins.

  • Measure: money spent before STOP
  • Who checks it: Finance
  • Compare with real decisions
03 · Timing

Decide before the field gets crowded

Finding an open opportunity earlier may avoid paying more later or arriving after competitors.

  • Measure: months to decision
  • Who checks it: Portfolio and BD
  • Initially only a scenario
04 · Better choices

Build a stronger mix of projects

Look at science, safety, patients, competition and Pfizer fit together instead of choosing on scientific association alone.

  • Measure: decisions and outcomes over time
  • Who checks it: Portfolio team
  • Needs longer-term evidence

Three honest ways to describe the result

Start conservatively. Claim more only after the pilot shows that TargetCompass caused the improvement.

Conservative case

It only saves time

TargetCompass makes preparation faster but does not change any decision. Count only the measured staff time saved.

Expected case

It saves time and supports one earlier STOP

Preparation is faster and one weak project is stopped earlier. Credit only a conservative portion to the tool.

Upside case

It also helps Pfizer move earlier

One promising target reaches a decision sooner. Put a number on this only after several real decisions show the effect.

When financial estimates become more detailedhow precision grows from target review to clinical signal▸

At the beginning, use broad ranges. Make the financial model more precise only as scientific evidence improves.

1 · TARGET REVIEW

Is it strategically interesting?

Use broad ranges for patients, competition, drug type and Pfizer fit. Fund the next experiment, not the whole future program.

2 · EARLY BIOLOGY PROOF

Is the next experiment worth funding?

Estimate the cost, time and chance that the next experiment will answer the most important question.

3 · DRUG CANDIDATE / FIRST HUMAN STUDY

What would development require?

Add expected development cost, timeline, technical success chance and the intended product profile.

4 · EARLY CLINICAL SIGNAL

Is there enough evidence for formal valuation?

Finance can now build more detailed scenarios for patients, access, price, share, manufacturing cost and risk-adjusted value.

Financial guardrail: TargetCompass organizes questions and assumptions; Finance owns the official valuation. No number should enter a decision deck without a named owner, date, range and source.
